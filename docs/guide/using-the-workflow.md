# Using the workflow

A module's stages open in order: a stage opens once every stage it builds on is complete.

## Reviewing a stage

A stage shows one tab per kind of item under its progress bar, and, whatever the number of kinds, to the right of them and apart from them, a tab for its coverages where it can hold any, a tab for its impacts where it builds on a stage, and last a **Resolved** tab for its resolved threads, latest resolved first. A tab you are not on shows how many of its units are not approved, so you can see which tab keeps the stage from completing. **Rules**, beside the stage's name, shows the shown kind's published rules: its question, its rules, the shared rules and its examples.

Sign each item, coverage and impact on its own. An item changed since you signed it shows how it differs, and **Restore** returns it to the version you signed. An item that is not approved names every reason: no signature, an open thread, out of date, or proposed for removal.

## Threads

**c** on a unit opens its thread; a further message while the thread is open adds to it. **New thread** in a stage's header, or **Shift+C**, opens a thread on the stage itself. Each message you send waits for an agent: the send button assigns it to the agent that last replied, or to the only available agent, and its menu to another agent or to none. The agent answers with a reply, or with a reply and changes to items. Only you resolve a thread.

The stage's header lists every open thread on the stage or its units, one row each: whom it waits for, what it is on and its last message. Rows waiting for you are orange. Open threads are listed by their last message, oldest first. A thread waiting for an agent that no agent has waits for you, to assign it. **Agents and threads** in the top bar, or **a**, lists every thread of every module in three groups, **Waiting for you**, **With an agent** and **Resolved**, with the agents in a row above them.

When a stage is empty, or a signature completes the stage it builds on, the stage offers **Ask the coding agent to write** it.

## Modules

A large product splits into modules, each with its own stages. **Cmd+Shift+M** shows the product as the project's name above its modules.
